## 4. Five standing rules on method

**These change how the remaining precondition work is done; they do not change what was asked in
§2.** Quoted rather than paraphrased, because a paraphrase of a rule is how the rule drifts.

1. **Run the migration end-to-end on every precondition PR.** *"`migrate()` on a `git archive`
   snapshot in a scratch directory is not the irreversible commit… From now on every PR touching
   `doc-id.py` runs a full `migrate()` on a snapshot of the real corpus and reports how far it
   got, by execution, with the write trace summed by family. **Pre-write-only replays are no
   longer evidence.** The `KeyError: 'RS'` and the fifth abort were each one snapshot run away
   from being found on the day F80–F82 cleared."*
2. **One discovery, two consumers.** *"`audit-docs.py` and `doc-id.py` each carry their own
   discovery, scope roots and exemption handling, and the fifth abort, F87 and F93 are all drift
   between the copies. Before the run, not after: extract discovery, `_ID_SCOPE_ROOTS`, and the
   exemption register into one module both scripts import, with one test that the two scripts see
   identical populations over the real corpus. This is a slice-sized job and it retires a finding
   class."*
3. **Documents are evidence, not the interface.** *"A decision document says what is asked, the
   measurement at one tree, and the recommendation — **under 300 lines**. Corrections to a filed
   record are one dated append, not a chain. Current state is read from `register-owed.py` and the
   roadmap row, never reconstructed from a closure record's §8. If you find yourself correcting
   your own correction, stop and file the state, not the history."*
4. **Batch the maintainer's decisions.** *"Do not route me one call at a time. The planner collects
   everything reserved to me, prices the options, and brings **one dated ruling document per
   day**; I fill it in one pass and it lands as the artifact. Chat is for questions, not
   decisions."*
5. **Convergence is measured.** *"Every slice close reports the count of new W37-6 preconditions
   it produced against the count it discharged."* The stopping rule of §2.4 stands.

**What is explicitly not changing**, in the maintainer's words: *"broken-input proofs, name-based
reconciliation, the predicate clause. Those found the defects. The waste is in the loop around
them, and these five close it."*
