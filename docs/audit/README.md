# docs/audit — the historical record

Created by the roadmap slim (NT-0009, accepted 2026-08-27). This directory holds what
`docs/roadmap.md` used to carry and no longer does: the closure records, the plan
reviews, and the retrofit-impossible list. The roadmap is the forward-looking plan;
this directory is the archive. Nothing here changes status — it is the record of what
was believed and decided at each date.

- `closure-records.md` — per-workstream closures, the Phase 1a exit demo, the independent
  audit, and the W5 slice records.
- `plan-reviews.md` — §14 plan reviews 1-6, with their proposals and acceptance lines.
- `retrofit-impossible.md` — the invariants the plan was shaped around.

The fuller audit structure proposed in NT-0008 — per-work-item and per-phase checklists
and registers — stays open until the maintainer answers its three acceptance points.
