# F87 — widening `_ID_SCOPE_ROOTS` does not widen the scope: the glob excludes 62 of the 65 exempt files

**Raised** 2026-09-02 by the lead, from a measurement by W37-5c item 3's executor. Work item
**W37-6**. Phase 2.

**The assumption this refutes was the lead's, stated repeatedly to the team on the day**: that
check 30 gets wired to the exemption register *when W37-6 widens `_ID_SCOPE_ROOTS`*. Widening the
roots is not sufficient, and nothing in the codebase said so until it was measured.

## The defect

`_id_scope_documents` (`scripts/audit-docs.py:1055-1063`) treats its two root kinds differently:

- a **file** root is taken verbatim — `files.append(root)`
- a **directory** root is expanded by **`files.extend(sorted(root.rglob("*.md")))`**

**So the glob, not the roots, is what bounds the scope.** A scope widened to `docs/` plus
`.claude/skills/`, `.claude/agents/` and `.claude/roles/` collects **markdown only**. Every
non-markdown file stays outside checks 30-39 no matter how the roots are set.

## Measured

**584 markdown files** are reachable under those four roots at `359936b`, predicate
`git ls-tree -r --name-only 359936b -- docs/ .claude/skills/ .claude/agents/ .claude/roles/`
filtered to `*.md` and de-duplicated. **Zero non-markdown files.**

The register holds **65** entries: **62 non-markdown** — 59 `.json` and 1 `.yaml` under
`docs/contracts/`, plus `docs/process/delivery-process.core.json` and
`docs/audit/file-census-5ef559d.csv` — and **3 markdown**, the unparseable vendored `SKILL.md`
manifests. A `*.md` glob therefore reaches **exactly 3 of 65**, and none of the 62.

**Pinned by** `tests/test_audit_docs_ids.py:1447`,
`test_widening_the_scope_roots_alone_reaches_no_non_markdown_file`, which passes on `main`.
`UNSTAMPABLE_EXEMPTIONS` (`scripts/audit-docs.py:2082`) is public for the W37-6 consumer, and the
comment block at `:1923-1928` records the wiring requirement in the code.

**The figure moved by one between two measurements and the reason is worth recording**: the
executor measured **583** at `f61f9a4`; this record measures **584** at `359936b`. The delta is
`docs/audit/findings/F86.md`, filed by the lead in between. Both figures are correct at their own
tree, which is why each carries one.

## Why this is worse than F80, F81 and F82 despite looking smaller

**Those three abort.** A real run halts, names the file, and the operator knows the migration did
not complete. **This one passes.**

Someone widens `_ID_SCOPE_ROOTS` in W37-6, watches checks 30-39 run green over 584 markdown
files, and ships — **having validated nothing at all over the 62 files the exemption was written
for, with every signal green.** The exemption register, the reason-and-ruling requirement, and
the symmetric-difference reconciliation that condition 2 bought are all real and all unreached.

**A guard that aborts announces itself. A scope that silently excludes does not.** That
distinction is the same one `F84` turns on, and it is why this is a register row rather than only
a passing test: a W37-6 planner reads the register; the test is read by whoever runs the suite,
after the decision it should have informed.

## Falsifiable

**Discharged when the scope selector reaches a non-markdown file**, proven on one of the 62 — not
when `_ID_SCOPE_ROOTS` is widened. The clause is deliberately on the **glob**, because widening
the roots is the change that looks like the fix and is not.

**Not discharged** by checks 30-39 passing after a root widening: that is the exact green this
finding says is uninformative. Re-opened if any scope selector in this family bounds itself by a
file extension without saying so in its own output.

W37-5c item 3 (`#634`, `359936b`). It asked *"what mutation would red this?"* of a **selector**
rather than an assert, found the answer was *none*, and pinned it from outside.
